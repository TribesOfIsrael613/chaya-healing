Good Questions

Frequently Asked

✿
Do I have to talk about painful things?

No. The body identifies what's held without you having to retell the story or relive it. Many people release emotions from events they don't consciously remember at all.

Does it work over the phone?

Yes. Energy work isn't limited by distance, and remote sessions are just as effective as sitting in the same room. Most clients do their sessions by phone or video.

How many sessions will I need?

It varies. Many people feel a real shift after one session. Longstanding patterns — a heart wall, chronic pain, years of food struggles — usually unwind over several sessions.

Can you work with children?

Yes, and children often respond very quickly. Sessions for children are done with a parent's consent and involvement.

Is this a replacement for medical care?

No — and it isn't meant to be. This work supports overall wellness alongside the care of your doctor. It is not a diagnosis, and nothing here should replace medical treatment or medication.

What's the Monthly Reset?

Ongoing monthly support rather than a single session — regular clearing so things don't build back up. Ask about it when you reach out.

Do I need any experience to attend the workshop?

None at all. The workshop is built for complete beginners, and you'll practice the whole process with guidance before you leave.
